What is Fixora?

FixoraPhone is a mobile repair and accessories service built to make getting help for your phone simpler, clearer and more convenient.

From describing the problem to booking a repair and checking its progress, our goal is to make the repair journey easier — with clear communication at every step.

We'll help you get back to what matters.
